Bartholomew, D. C., Banin, L. F., Bittencourt, P. R. L., Suis, M. A. F., Mercado, L. M., Nilus, R., Burslem, D. F. R. P., Rowland, L. R. Differential nutrient limitation and tree height control leaf physiology, supporting niche partitioning in tropical dipterocarp forests; Functional Ecology.

This dataset is used for analyses published in Bartholomew et al. (2022) Functional Ecology. The dataset includes the following traits:
height		Tree Height									(m)
Asat_area	Asat (Photosynthesis under saturating light and ambient CO2) on an area basis	(µmol m-2 s-1)
Asat_mass	Asat (Photosynthesis under saturating light and ambient CO2) on a mass basis	(µmol kg-1 s-1)
Vcmax_area	Vcmax (Maximum rate of carboxylation) on an area basis				(µmol m-2 s-1)
Vcmax_mass	Vcmax (Maximum rate of carboxylation) on a mass basis				(µmol kg-1 s-1)
Rleaf_area	Rleaf (Dark adapted leaf respiration) on an area basis				(µmol m-2 s-1)
Rleaf_mass	Rleaf (Dark adapted leaf respiration) on a mass basis				(µmol kg-1 s-1)
LMA		Leaf mass per area								(g m-2)
leaf.thickness	Leaf thickness									(mm)
gdark		Minimum stomatal conductance							(mol m-2 s-1)
n.leaf		Leaf nitrogen concentration on a mass basis					(g kg-1)
p.leaf		Leaf phosphorus concentration on a mass basis					(g kg-1)
ca.leaf		Leaf calcium concentration on a mass basis					(g kg-1)
k.leaf		Leaf potassium concentration on a mass basis					(g kg-1)
mg.leaf		Leaf magnesium concentration on a mass basis					(g kg-1)
n.area		Leaf nitrogen concentration on an area basis					(g m-2)
p.area		Leaf phosphorus concentration on an area basis					(g m-2)
ca.area		Leaf calcium concentration on an area basis					(g m-2)
k.area		Leaf potassium concentration on an area basis					(g m-2)
mg.area		Leaf magnesium concentration on an area basis					(g m-2)

The dataset includes information on the location of the tree individuals within the permanent 4ha plots established within the Kabili-Sepilok Forest Reserve, Sabah, Malaysia.
The species, forest type, generalist/specialist classification used within the analysis of Bartholomew et al. (2022) are presented.
Dates of measurements are included.
